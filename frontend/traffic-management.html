THE CHALLENGE

Traffic management faces real-world emergency challenges.

Without live awareness of emergency vehicle locations and coordinated route tools, traffic management cannot support emergency response effectively.

Traffic Congestion

Emergency vehicles encounter dense urban bottlenecks without coordinated green corridor clearance.

Ambulance Delays

Sirens and lights alone cannot clear congested intersections, causing critical transport delays.

No Emergency Vehicle Awareness

Traffic control centers lack real-time visibility into active sirens and emergency routes.

Delayed Manual Coordination

Manual phone calls between ambulance dispatchers and traffic police arrive too late to alter signals.

Isolated Traffic Systems

Traffic management and emergency dispatch operate on disconnected platforms with zero sync.

Limited Performance Analytics

Cities lack data on emergency transit times, delay bottlenecks, and intersection congestion impact.

HOW EMSTRAP HELPS

Intelligent corridor preemption and transit synchronization.

EMSTRAP bridges traffic management centers directly with emergency vehicles for zero-delay urban transit.

Emergency Route Awareness

Live display of inbound ambulance paths on traffic management consoles.

Corridor Coordination

Dynamic signal sequencing ensures green lights ahead of approaching emergency sirens.

Traffic Signal Preemption

Automated green wave preemption clears congested intersections seamlessly.

Real-Time Vehicle Tracking

High-precision GPS telemetry streams emergency vehicle speed and exact intersection proximity.

Transit Analytics & Reporting

Actionable reports measuring response speedups, green wave efficiency, and intersection clearance performance.
