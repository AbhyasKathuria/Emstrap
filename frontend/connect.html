SYSTEMIC CHALLENGE

The Problem: Disconnected Emergency Response

When an emergency occurs, information can come from multiple sources—citizens, police officers, control rooms, security teams, or people at the scene. Without a unified system, authorities face critical operational bottlenecks.

01

Duplicate Incident Chaos

Multiple reports about the same incident crowd lines and fracture operator attention.

02

Unclear Scene Context

Incomplete or vague caller statements delay medical triage and asset preparation.

03

Sharing Latency

Manual phone calls between dispatch desks create critical communication gaps.

04

Vague Location Data

Difficulty identifying exact GPS coordinates of caller in chaotic environments.

05

Inter-Agency Gaps

Siloed systems prevent police, fire, ambulances, and municipalities from co-viewing incidents.

06

Triage Blindspots

Difficulty prioritizing simultaneous emergencies without automated severity scoring.

EMSTRAP Connect brings these fragmented reports into one connected emergency-response ecosystem.

CORE ARCHITECTURE

What is EMSTRAP Connect?

EMSTRAP Connect acts as a digital communication bridge between citizens and government emergency-response systems. A citizen reports an emergency with rich structured telemetry:

Location (Sub-second Lat / Lng)
Description & Acuity
Scene Image & Evidence
Time of Occurrence & Details

When multiple people report the same incident, EMSTRAP identifies reports coming from the same location and associates them with a single incident, reducing unnecessary duplicate cases.

END-TO-END WORKFLOW

How EMSTRAP Connect Works

The 8-step incident lifecycle from initial citizen trigger to final case resolution.

01 // REPORT

Report Emergency

Emergency type, description, live image, and incident details submitted digitally.

02 // LOCATE

Precise Coordinates

Captures latitude and longitude to pinpoint the exact scene location.

03 // IDENTIFY

Duplicate Detection

Checks if active incident exists nearby and clusters 5 reports into 1 case.

04 // ANALYZE

AI-Assisted Audit

Extracts severity, trauma indications, and asset needs from unstructured data.

05 // PRIORITIZE

Urgency Staging

Assigns priority: Critical → High → Medium → Low for immediate action.

06 // CONNECT

Agency Dispatch

Connects case to the right authority, department, or medical response unit.

07 // COORDINATE

Active Response

Authorized personnel monitor progress, green lights, and team coordination.

08 // RESOLVE

Incident Closure

Monitors through the lifecycle until safely resolved, closed, and logged.
