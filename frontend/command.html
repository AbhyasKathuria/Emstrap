B2B PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

What is EMSTRAP Command?

EMSTRAP Command is the B2B operational platform within the EMSTRAP ecosystem. It is designed specifically for:

🚑 Ambulance Providers

Manage ambulance fleets, receive bookings, coordinate trips and monitor active patient transportation requests digitally.

🏥 Hospitals

Manage ambulance requests, receive incoming patient information in advance, and coordinate inter-facility patient transfers seamlessly.

🏥 Healthcare Networks

Organizations managing multiple hospitals or healthcare facilities can use Command to coordinate transportation and emergency-related activity across locations.

CLINICAL PRE-HOSPITAL COORDINATION

🏥 Command for Hospitals

Prepare for the patient before arrival.

Hospitals can use EMSTRAP Command to receive and manage relevant patient transportation and emergency information in advance, enabling emergency room teams and trauma bays to prepare before the patient reaches the hospital.

Hospital Dashboard

A hospital's Command dashboard provides complete clinical visibility into:

• Incoming ambulances & ETA
• Expected patient arrivals
• Patient transportation requests
• Emergency medical context
• Hospital transfer requests & history
Enables ED physicians and triage nurses to pre-allocate beds, prepare resuscitation equipment, and eliminate intake bottlenecks.

Transfers & Emergency Sync

Hospital-to-Hospital Transfer:
Hospital A → Transfer Request → EMSTRAP Command → Available Ambulance → Hospital B

Coordinate transfers without relying entirely on manual phone calls.

Emergency Hospital Coordination:
Report → EMSTRAP → Command → Ambulance → Hospital Notified → Arrival

Creates a connected journey from incident to transportation to hospital arrival.

Seamless integration with EMSTRAP Connect, Ride, and Shield incident feeds.
OPERATIONAL LIFECYCLE

🔄 Complete Command Workflow

From Request to Resolution: A structured 10-step lifecycle.

STEP 01

Request Received

Arrives from Ride, Shield, Connect, Hospital, or authorized sources.

STEP 02

Request Reviewed

Ambulance provider reviews patient requirement and pickup details.

STEP 03

Ambulance Identified

Available ambulances and proximity grid are checked.

STEP 04

Ambulance Assigned

Appropriate ambulance vehicle and crew are locked for dispatch.

STEP 05

Pickup

Ambulance travels to pickup location with real-time GPS tracking.

STEP 06

Patient Picked Up

Medical crew assists patient onboard; trip status updates.

STEP 07

Hospital Coordination

Destination hospital receives advance clinical & arrival notices.

STEP 08

Patient Transport

Continuous monitoring and safe transit to receiving facility.

STEP 09

Hospital Arrival

Patient reaches destination and medical handover begins.

STEP 10

Trip Completed

Trip is closed and permanent digital audit log is maintained.

OPERATIONAL DEPTH

Comprehensive Operational Modules

Built for high-volume emergency management, fleet optimization, and multi-hospital healthcare networks.

🚑 Fleet Management

Registration, ambulance type (BLS/ALS/ICU), availability, assigned trips, service history, and operational GPS telemetry.

👨‍⚕️ Driver & Crew Management

Maintain operational details about paramedic staff, drivers, assigned vehicle, active shifts, and historical trip logs.

📍 Location-Based Operations

Answers the three critical questions in emergency transit: Where is the ambulance? Where is the patient? Where is the patient going?

📋 Booking Lifecycle

Pending → Accepted → Assigned → En Route → Arrived → Patient Onboard → In Transit → Completed.

📑 Structured Trip Records

Every completed trip maintains Booking ID, Pickup, Destination, Ambulance, Crew, Duration, and Handover timestamps.

📊 Reports & Analytics

Understand ambulance utilization rates, average response intervals, cancellation metrics, and inter-facility transfer volumes.
