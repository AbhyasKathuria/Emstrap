🏥 Hospital Coordination

When an employee or covered family member requires medical attention, EMSTRAP supports coordination with the receiving hospital as part of the emergency workflow (sharing emergency details, patient transfer coordination, and status tracking).

Important Boundary Distinction

Hospital coordination is not the same as hospital expense coverage. EMSTRAP does not automatically pay for hospital admission, doctor consultations, surgery, treatment, medicines, diagnostic tests, or ICU charges. Those are handled separately by the patient under their own insurance or medical coverage.

• EMSTRAP → Covers eligible ambulance cost • Hospital → Medical/treatment expenses are separate
