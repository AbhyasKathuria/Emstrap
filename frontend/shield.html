PREVENTION & READINESS

Why EMSTRAP Shield?

Workplace safety doesn't begin when an emergency happens. A truly safe organization needs comprehensive protection:

Prepare Conduct drills, risk assessments, and first-aid readiness before an emergency.
Respond Immediate SOS dispatch, rapid ambulance coordination, and hospital pre-alerts.
Learn Post-incident audit logs, response time telemetry, and protocol compliance reports.
THE PROBLEM

Finding an ambulance shouldn't become another emergency.

When an emergency strikes in an enterprise or campus, staff often face critical delays:

Searching for nearby ambulances
Making multiple phone calls
Checking fleet availability
Repeating patient condition
Waiting endlessly for confirmation
Manual campus gate coordination

During high-stress corporate emergencies, every lost minute risks employee lives and business continuity.
