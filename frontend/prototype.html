Proof of Concept

Functional Prototype

From Concept to a Working Prototype. The functional prototype successfully validated the core concept behind EMSTRAP by integrating emergency reporting, stakeholder communication, location intelligence, and real-time coordination into a unified digital platform.

Development Objectives

The primary objective of the prototype was to verify that technology could simplify and strengthen emergency response operations by enabling seamless communication between all stakeholders involved in an emergency.

Validate the feasibility of the EMSTRAP concept.
Demonstrate real-time emergency reporting.
Improve communication between emergency stakeholders.
Enable intelligent incident management.
Provide real-time situational awareness.
Create a scalable foundation for future deployment.

Prototype Capabilities

Smart Reporting

Quick SOS triggers transmitting exact coordinates and scene descriptions.

Location Intelligence

Live GPS tracking of emergency vehicles and responders on active routes.

Hospital Pre-Alerts

Direct alerts with pre-arrival telemetry sent to triage staff.

AI-Assisted Decisions

Smart vehicle allocation and standby placement recommendations.

Prototype Workflow

Step 1

Emergency Report

Citizen submits emergency incident report via mobile app

Step 2

Location Captured

System automatically captures exact GPS coordinates

Step 3

AI Analysis

AI triage filters incident severity and recommends dispatch

Step 4

Alert Generated

Instant high-priority dispatch notice broadcasted

Step 5

Stakeholder Sync

Emergency responders, ER triage, and police synchronized

Step 6

Real-Time Monitor

Continuous live route telemetry tracked in control room

Step 7

Emergency Response

Ambulance lands at site for critical medical transport

Prototype Architecture

The functional prototype was designed using a modular and scalable architecture capable of supporting future expansion and integration with additional emergency response systems.
