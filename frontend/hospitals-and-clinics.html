OPERATIONAL CHALLENGES

Hospital emergency departments face real-world intake bottlenecks.

Without real-time inbound telemetry and ETA tracking, emergency rooms lose precious golden-hour minutes preparing for critical trauma arrivals.

Unannounced Trauma Arrivals

Emergency rooms receive sudden critical arrivals without advance clinical telemetry, leading to chaotic intake queues.

Fragmented Radio Handoffs

Static phone calls and verbal radio handovers cause clinical errors and delayed transmission of vital patient signs.

Trauma Bay & Bed Delays

Trauma bays, CT scanners, and ICU beds are allocated only after the ambulance arrives at the hospital bay.

Delayed Specialist Mobilization

On-call surgeons, cardiologists, and stroke teams are paged late, delaying emergency interventions.

Paper Handoff Inefficiencies

Manual paper documentation between paramedics and ER triage nurses slows patient admission and care initiation.

Incomplete Pre-Hospital Data

Without digital records and operational data, providers cannot measure performance or identify systemic inefficiencies.

INTEGRATED SOLUTIONS

A connected platform for smarter ambulance operations.

EMSTRAP integrates every stage of the ambulance emergency lifecycle — from request intake to patient handover and post-incident reporting.

Intelligent Emergency Dispatch

Automated emergency intake with smart assignment of the nearest available ambulance based on live fleet data.

Real-Time GPS Tracking

Live location monitoring of every ambulance in the fleet from a centralized dispatch dashboard.

Smart Routing

Route optimization and traffic coordination to minimize response times for every active emergency.

Hospital Coordination

Automatic advance notifications to receiving hospitals with patient status and estimated arrival time.

Driver Dashboard

Mobile-first driver interface with navigation, job status updates and direct communication tools.

Operational Analytics

Trip records, response time data and fleet utilization reports to support operational improvement.
