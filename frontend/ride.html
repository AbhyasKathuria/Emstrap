When a patient needs transportation, families often have to:

× Search for nearby ambulances
× Make multiple phone calls
× Check availability
× Explain the patient's requirements repeatedly
× Wait for confirmation
× Coordinate pickup and destination manually

During a stressful situation, this process can be difficult.

How EMSTRAP Ride Works?

STEP 01

Enter Pickup Location

Provide where the patient needs to be picked up.

STEP 02

Enter Destination

Provide the destination or another facility.

STEP 03

Select Ambulance Requirement

Choose the appropriate ambulance type based on patient needs.

STEP 04

Request an Ambulance

Submit the booking request.

STEP 05

Ambulance Matching

Emergency & Non-Emergency Transportation

For life-threatening emergencies, contact the appropriate emergency number immediately.
