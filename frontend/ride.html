The Problem

Finding an ambulance shouldn't become another emergency.

When a patient needs transportation, families often have to face immense friction during a stressful situation:

✕

Search for nearby ambulances

Scrambling through online listings, directories, and scattered contacts.

✕

Make multiple phone calls

Calling provider after provider hoping someone answers immediately.

✕

Check availability

Hearing ambulances are already occupied or out of designated service areas.

✕

Explain patient requirements repeatedly

Repeating medical condition, oxygen needs, and stretcher specs to every operator.

✕

Wait for confirmation

Anxious waiting with no clear confirmation or expected arrival timeline.

✕

Coordinate pickup and destination manually

Describing complex landmarks over voice calls with no live GPS tracking.

CORE PLATFORM

What is EMSTRAP Ride?

EMSTRAP Ride allows the general public to book an ambulance for patient transportation through a simple digital process.

A USER CAN PROVIDE:

Simple, structured information to ensure an exact medical match:

Pickup Location Home or hospital ward
Destination Hospital or medical center
Patient Requirements Oxygen, stretcher, monitor
Preferred Ambulance Type BLS, ALS, or Patient Van
Contact Information Direct caregiver phone
Additional Trip Details Attendant seats & floor

Intelligent Matching: The platform then helps connect the request with an available ambulance service.

VERSATILE FLEET CAPABILITIES

Emergency & Non-Emergency Transportation

EMSTRAP Ride can support different types of patient transportation needs across the entire continuum of healthcare.

Emergency Transportation

Urgent Medical Dispatch

For situations where immediate medical transportation is required, users can request an ambulance through the platform.

Important Notice

For life-threatening emergencies, users should still contact the appropriate emergency number (112 / 108) immediately. EMSTRAP Ride should not be presented as a replacement for emergency dispatch unless officially integrated and authorized for that purpose.

Non-Emergency Patient Transport

Planned & Scheduled Trips

EMSTRAP Ride can also be useful when a patient needs transportation but is not experiencing an immediate life-threatening emergency.

Common Examples:
Hospital discharge
Hospital-to-hospital transfer
Home-to-hospital transportation
Medical appointments
Follow-up visits
Patient relocation
Elderly or mobility-limited patient transportation
INTELLIGENT MATCHING

Smart Ambulance Booking

Find the right ride for the patient.

An ambulance is not simply another vehicle. Different patients may have different transportation requirements. EMSTRAP Ride is designed to capture the information needed to help identify an appropriate ambulance.

THE MATCHING PROCESS CAN CONSIDER:

Patient Requirement

What type of transportation is needed?

Location

Where is the patient?

Destination

Where does the patient need to go?

Availability

Which suitable ambulance is available?

Distance

Which available option can serve the request?
